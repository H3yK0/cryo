A home-screen widget, at any size, showing what you choose. Lock again after the time you pick (immediately, 1, 5 or 15 min) and privacy mode (screenshots come out black). Swipe sideways to change tabs. Arrange each screen: reorder blocks or hide the ones you don't use. Insights: health on top, the money flow in one box as Bars or Funnel, and a tap opens the transactions. The heat calendar in temperature colours. Crash fixes.
